🌿 FloraFlow — Garden Planning, Grounded in Nature and Backed by AI

FloraFlow is a garden design and restoration app for anyone with a patch of soil to plant — a sunny balcony, a backyard bed, or a full landscape. Plan your layout, get plant-specific advice from an AI garden assistant, preview your design in AR, and unwind with a Restoration Journal built from real nature sound and brainwave audio.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

🌱 Plan your layout
Sketch your garden on an interactive grid canvas. Place plants, size your beds, and set companion-planting boundaries. FloraFlow flags incompatible pairings before you plant them and suggests companions that support pollination, deter pests, and help your garden grow together well.

🧠 Ask your AI garden assistant
Chat with FloraFlow's Gemini-powered garden assistant about your specific space. It asks about your goals, sun and soil conditions, and maintenance preferences, then recommends plants and layouts — and explains why they work, so you learn as you plan.

📸 Preview your garden in AR
See your design come to life with FloraFlow's AR Lens. View plants at different growth stages, from seedling to mature, and switch between weather overlays like gentle rain, cherry blossom drift, and fireflies at dusk. Save snapshots of your layout straight to your gallery.

📚 Look up any plant
Browse a companion-planting library with 100+ plant profiles covering sunlight needs, soil pH, water requirements, and which neighbors help (or hurt) each other. Set reminders for watering, fertilizing, and pruning by plant.

🎧 Unwind with the Restoration Journal
Put on your headphones. The Restoration Journal pairs procedural binaural beats (Alpha, Theta, and Delta) with layered nature soundscapes — wind chimes, rustling leaves — generated around your own garden plan, not generic ambient loops. Log your time in the garden and your mood before and after, and see how tending your plants and your Restoration score connect over time.

📊 Your dashboard, at a glance
Check in on your Biophilic Profile, Companion Synergy, and Weather Sync cards to see how your garden and your restoration habits are trending.
