# FloraFlow Creator Seed Kit: "The Restorative Movement"

This kit provides the framework for seeding FloraFlow with high-quality "Slow Living," "Interior Design," and "Wellness" creators. Our goal is to move away from "ad-heavy" content toward **authentic environmental integration**.

---

## 1. Target Creator Personas

| Persona | Aesthetic | Key Value Prop |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Slow Living Minimalist** | Muted tones, neutral textures, morning rituals, "cottagecore" leanings. | Focus on the **Restoration Journal** and the "morning ritual" of planning a garden. |
| **The Biophilic Interior Designer** | High-end, curated, focus on lighting and plant placement. | Focus on the **2D Garden Planner** and the **AI Garden Counsel** as a professional tool. |
| **The Neuro-Diversity / ADHD Advocate** | Honest, relatable, focus on focus/productivity/overstimulation. | Focus on the **Neural Load Audit** and "filtering out the noise." |

---

## 2. Outreach Templates

### A. The "Soft" Initial Reach-out (DM/Email)
*Goal: Start a conversation, not sell a product.*

> "Hi [Name], I’ve been following your content on [Platform] and absolutely love how you curate [specific aesthetic, e.g., your 'slow morning' rituals / biophilic corner]. 
>
> I'm currently building FloraFlow—an app designed to help people lower their 'Neural Load' through biophilic design. Given your eye for [Aesthetic], I’d love to get your early feedback on our 2D Garden Planner and the AI Garden Counsel. 
>
> Would you be open to trying out the app first and letting me know what you think? No strings attached—just looking for honest feedback from people who truly 'get' the restorative vibe."

### B. The "Value" Follow-up (For interested creators)
*Goal: Propose a collaboration.*

> "Thanks for checking out the app! We're looking for a few core partners to help us launch the 'Restorative Movement.' 
>
> We’d love to offer you a [Pro/Lifetime] account and potentially a unique code for your community. In return, we’re looking for a few authentic 'walkthroughs'—not a hard sell, but a look at how you use the Garden Planner or the Restoration Journal as part of your daily routine.
>
> Let me know if you'd be interested in a formal partnership!"

---

## 3. The Creator Brief (The "Do's & Don'ts")

**The Vibe:** *Restorative, Immersive, Guided Exploration.*

### ✅ DO:
*   **Show the "Vibe":** Use natural lighting, soft textures, and "lo-fi" transitions.
*   **Highlight the AI Counsel:** Show the AI "thinking" and providing specific, sophisticated advice (not just generic plant names).
*   **Focus on the "Flow":** Show the satisfaction of the 2D Planner—the "Zen" of arranging a space.
*   **Mention "Neural Load":** Use it as a way to describe the feeling of being "overstimulated" by a busy room.

### ❌ DON'T:
*   **High-Energy/Hype:** Avoid fast, loud cuts or "shouty" voiceovers. This breaks the restorative brand voice.
*   **Generic "Plant Tips":** Don't make it about "how to keep a fern alive." Make it about "how to design a sanctuary."
*   **Cluttered Screens:** Don't show messy UI or too many notifications.

---

## 4. Content "Seed" Ideas
1.  **"Plan with Me":** A time-lapse of the creator using the 2D Planner to design a "Reading Nook" or "Home Office Sanctuary."
2.  **"The 3 PM Reset":** A video showing a creator feeling drained, doing a "Neural Load Audit," and then spending 5 minutes in the app planning a biophilic escape.
3.  **"Morning Rituals":** A "Get Ready With Me" (GRWM) style video where the creator uses the Restoration Journal to set their intention for the day.

---

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
*   **Primary:** Unique Promo Code Redeemed (Attribution).
*   **Secondary:** "Save" Rate (How many people saved the creator's content for inspiration).
*   **Tertiary:** User Feedback (Qualitative comments on the "Restorative" experience).
