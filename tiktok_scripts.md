# FloraFlow TikTok Marketing Scripts - Batch 1: "The Wow Moments"

## Strategy Overview
These scripts are designed to capture attention in the first 3 seconds by highlighting the most unique and "high-fidelity" features of the FloraFlow app: the AI Garden Counsel and the 2D Garden Planner.

---

## Script 1: The "Lazy" Gardener (AI Garden Counsel)
*   **Goal:** Show how easy it is to get professional-level biophilic advice.
*   **Visuals:** Split screen. Top: Someone looking stressed at a messy, empty room. Bottom: Screen recording of the FloraFlow "Garden Counsel" chat.
*   **Hook (0-3s):** (TOS: "Stop guessing where your plants go. 🌿")
*   **Audio/VO:** "I wanted a sanctuary, but I have zero experience with biophilic design. I just told the FloraFlow AI Counsel my room dimensions and my vibe—'Mist-covered forest'—and it literally gave me a custom plan."
*   **Body:** (Fast cuts of the AI responding with specific plant types and layout suggestions).
*   **CTA:** (TOS: "Design your sanctuary in the link in bio.")

---

## Script 2: 60-Second Sanctuary (2D Garden Planner)
*   **Goal:** High-speed visual transformation.
*   **Visuals:** Rapid "Time-lapse" style recording of the 2D Garden Planner. Start with an empty grid, and watch as plants, paths, and textures are added rapidly.
*   **Hook (0-3s):** (TOS: "Watch me build a sanctuary in 60 seconds. ✨")
*   **Audio/VO:** "This is how I plan my mental escape. The 2D planner is so satisfying to use because you can see the flow of the space before you ever buy a single leaf."
*   **Body:** (Music swells as the garden becomes lush and beautiful).
*   **CTA:** (TOS: "Try the 2D Planner on FloraFlow.")

## Script 3: The "Brain Fog" Fix (Neural Load Audit)
*   **Goal:** Connect the "Science" to the "App."
