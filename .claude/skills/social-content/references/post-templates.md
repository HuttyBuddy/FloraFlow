# Post Templates & Extended Hook Library

## LinkedIn Post Templates

### The Lesson Story
```
[Hook: surprising outcome or admission]

[Context: what the situation was]

[Turning point: what happened / what you realized]

[Lesson: the takeaway, stated plainly]

[Optional: how this applies to the reader]
```

### The Framework Breakdown
```
[Hook: promise of the framework, e.g. "Here's the 3-step process I use to..."]

1. [Step one + 1-2 sentence explanation]
2. [Step two + 1-2 sentence explanation]
3. [Step three + 1-2 sentence explanation]

[Closing: when/why this works, or an invitation to discuss]
```

### The Contrarian Take
```
[Hook: "Unpopular opinion:" or "Everyone says X. I think that's wrong."]

[Reasoning: 2-3 short paragraphs explaining your position]

[Acknowledge the counterargument briefly]

[Closing: restate your position confidently, invite discussion]
```

## Twitter/X Thread Templates

### The Takeaway Thread
```
1/ [Standalone hook tweet — must work without the rest of the thread]

2/ [Setup: why this topic matters]

3-N/ [One idea per tweet, numbered, each short and punchy]

Last/ [Summary + CTA: follow, reply, or link to more]
```

### The "I Was Wrong" Thread
```
1/ I used to believe [common belief]. I was wrong.

2/ Here's what changed my mind: [story or data]

3-N/ [Supporting points]

Last/ [What you believe now, and why it matters]
```

## Instagram Carousel Templates

### The Swipe-Through Framework
```
Slide 1: [Hook headline — bold, large text, promise of value]
Slide 2: [Problem/context — why this matters]
Slide 3-N: [One point per slide — number them, e.g. "1 of 5"]
Slide Last: [Recap + CTA — "Save this for later" / "Follow for more"]
```

### The Before/After Carousel
```
Slide 1: [Hook — "How we went from X to Y"]
Slide 2: [The "before" state, with specifics]
Slide 3: [What changed — the method/decision]
Slide 4: [The "after" state, with specifics]
Slide 5: [How the reader can apply this]
```

## Extended Hook Formulas

### Numbers & Lists
- "5 [things] that [outcome] (most people get #3 wrong)"
- "I tried [X] for [time period]. Here's what happened."
- "[Number]% of [audience] don't know this about [topic]."

### Direct Address
- "If you're [specific audience], read this."
- "You're doing [common task] wrong. Here's the fix."
- "Stop scrolling if you [specific pain point]."

### Comparison
- "[Old way] vs. [new way] — the difference will surprise you."
- "Most people do [A]. The top performers do [B]."

### Question Hooks
- "What if [common assumption] is completely backwards?"
- "Why does [surprising thing] keep happening?"

## Call-to-Action (CTA) Library

**Engagement CTAs:**
- "What's your experience with this? Drop it below."
- "Agree or disagree? Let me know."
- "Tag someone who needs to see this."

**Save/Share CTAs:**
- "Save this for your next [task]."
- "Share this with someone on your team."

**Follow CTAs:**
- "Follow for more on [topic]."
- "Part 2 tomorrow — follow so you don't miss it."

**Conversion CTAs (use sparingly, ~5% of content):**
- "Link in bio/comments if you want to try it."
- "DM me '[keyword]' and I'll send you [resource]."
