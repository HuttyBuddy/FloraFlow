# Platform Strategies — Detailed Guide

## LinkedIn

**What works:**
- Personal stories with a business lesson
- Carousels (PDF documents) breaking down a framework, step-by-step
- "I learned this the hard way" posts
- Native video under 2 minutes
- Polls for engagement and audience research

**Posting cadence:** 3-5x/week for individuals, 1x/day max for company pages.

**Format notes:**
- First 2-3 lines must work as a hook before "see more" truncation
- Line breaks improve readability — avoid wall-of-text paragraphs
- Tag people only when genuinely relevant (over-tagging reads as spammy)
- Native documents (carousels) currently get strong algorithmic boost

**Avoid:** External links in the post body (reduces reach) — put links in the first comment instead.

## Twitter/X

**What works:**
- Threads that open with a strong, standalone hook tweet
- Hot takes / contrarian opinions
- Real-time commentary on industry news
- Quote-tweeting with added insight
- Data-driven "stat flash" tweets

**Posting cadence:** 3-10x/day for active accounts (mix of original posts, replies, and reposts).

**Format notes:**
- The first tweet of a thread must work standalone — it's what gets shared/quoted
- Reply to your own thread to extend it rather than packing everything into one tweet
- Engagement in the first 15-30 minutes heavily influences distribution

**Avoid:** Posting and immediately leaving — replying to early comments boosts algorithmic reach.

## Instagram

**What works:**
- Carousels (especially "swipe for more" educational content)
- Reels with strong hooks in the first second
- Stories for behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&A
- Before/after and transformation content

**Posting cadence:** 1-2 feed posts/day, multiple Stories/day.

**Format notes:**
- Saves and shares are weighted heavily — design carousels people want to bookmark
- First frame of a carousel/Reel is the thumbnail — make it stand alone
- Captions support the visual; don't rely on caption to carry the message

**Avoid:** Low-resolution images, watermarks from other platforms (e.g. TikTok watermark on a Reel) — these reduce reach.

---

## TikTok

**What works:**
- Raw, authentic, lower-production content often outperforms polished video
- Trends and sounds — but adapted to your niche, not generic participation
- "POV" and relatable-scenario formats
- Series content (Part 1/2/3) to drive follows

**Posting cadence:** 1-4x/day for growth accounts.

**Format notes:**
- Hook must land in the first 1-3 seconds — viewers swipe fast
- Native text overlay and captions matter more than the written caption
- Comments are part of the content — pin a comment to add context or humor

**Avoid:** Overly polished "ad-like" content — TikTok's algorithm and audience favor native-feeling video.

---

## Facebook

**What works:**
- Native video (uploaded directly, not linked from YouTube)
- Community/group-style posts that invite discussion
- Local business updates, events, and offers
- Longer-form storytelling posts perform better here than on Twitter/X

**Posting cadence:** 1-2x/day for pages; groups can sustain more frequent posting.

**Format notes:**
- Groups generally outperform Pages for organic reach
- Questions and "fill in the blank" posts drive comments

**Avoid:** Link posts to external sites — these are deprioritized; share the value natively and link in comments if needed.

---

## YouTube Shorts

**What works:**
- Searchable, keyword-rich titles (Shorts are indexed like long-form YouTube)
- Hooks that promise a payoff visible in the thumbnail/first frame
- Loops — content that re-watches well when it ends and restarts seamlessly

**Posting cadence:** 1x/day is sustainable for most creators; consistency matters more than volume.

**Format notes:**
- Unlike TikTok/Reels, Shorts benefit from SEO — treat titles like mini blog post titles
- Shorts can drive subscribers to long-form content — include a soft CTA when relevant
