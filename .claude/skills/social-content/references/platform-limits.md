# Platform Limits — Character Counts & Hashtags

Quick reference for copy length and hashtag conventions. Always check current platform docs before relying on exact numbers — these change periodically.

## Character / Length Limits

| Platform | Post Limit | Practical Sweet Spot | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | 150-300 chars for feed posts; up to 1,300 for "see more" posts | Posts under ~210 chars don't get a "see more" truncation |
| Twitter/X | 280 characters (25,000 for Premium long-form) | 70-100 chars for highest engagement | Threads: each tweet counts separately |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters | First 125 chars shown before "more" | Bio limited to 150 chars |
| TikTok | 2,200 characters (caption) | 1-2 short sentences | On-screen text overlay is separate and more important than caption |
| Facebook | 63,206 characters | 40-80 chars for link posts | Shorter posts get more organic reach |
| YouTube (Shorts/description) | 5,000 characters (description), 100 chars (title) | Title front-loaded with keywords | Shorts captions follow on-screen text rules |

## Hashtag Guidance

| Platform | Recommended Count | Placement | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 3-5 | End of post | Avoid generic tags like #motivation; favor niche/industry tags |
| Twitter/X | 0-2 | Inline or end | Heavy hashtag use reduces perceived authenticity |
| Instagram | 3-8 (up to 30 allowed) | End of caption or first comment | Mix broad + niche + branded tags |
| TikTok | 3-5 | End of caption | Include 1 broad discovery tag + niche tags + trend tag if relevant |
| Facebook | 0-2 | Inline | Hashtags have minimal effect on reach |

## Image / Video Specs Quick Reference

| Platform | Feed Image | Video Aspect | Max Video Length (organic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 1200x627 (link), 1080x1080 (square) | 1:1 or 4:5 | 10 min (clips perform best under 2 min) |
| Twitter/X | 1200x675 | 16:9 or 1:1 | 2 min 20 sec |
| Instagram Feed | 1080x1080 or 1080x1350 | 4:5 or 1:1 | 60 sec (Reels up to 90 sec) |
| Instagram Reels | n/a | 9:16 | 90 sec |
| TikTok | n/a | 9:16 | 10 min (best performance 15-60 sec) |
| YouTube Shorts | n/a | 9:16 | 60 sec |
