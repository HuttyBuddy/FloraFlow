# Reverse Engineering Viral Content — Complete Framework

A systematic process for identifying what works in your niche and adapting it to your voice, instead of guessing or copying directly.

## Step 1: Find Creators

Identify 10-20 accounts that:
- Operate in your niche or an adjacent one
- Have engagement rates noticeably above the platform average
- Post consistently (enough volume to find patterns, not one-hit-wonders)

Mix of:
- Direct competitors / peers
- Larger accounts you aspire toward
- Smaller accounts with surprisingly high engagement relative to follower count (often signals strong format-market fit)

## Step 2: Collect Data

For each creator, gather their last 25-50 posts (aim for 500+ total across the set). For each post, record:
- Format (text post, carousel, thread, video, etc.)
- Hook (first line / first 3 seconds, verbatim)
- Topic/theme
- Length
- Engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares/reposts, saves if visible)
- Posting day/time if available

A simple spreadsheet works well: one row per post, columns for the above.

## Step 3: Analyze Patterns

Sort by engagement and look for repeats among the top 10-20% of posts:

- **Hook patterns** — Do top posts share a structure? (e.g. "I was wrong about X", numbered lists, contrarian openers)
- **Format patterns** — Is one format consistently outperforming others for this creator?
- **Topic patterns** — Are certain themes (e.g. failure stories, data callouts, tactical how-tos) overrepresented at the top?
- **Length patterns** — Shorter or longer than their average?
- **Timing patterns** — Any correlation with day/time posted?

Separate signal from noise: a single viral outlier doesn't make a pattern. Look for things that repeat across multiple top performers and multiple creators.

## Step 4: Codify the Playbook

Turn the patterns into a short, reusable list, e.g.:

```
For [niche], top-performing posts tend to:
- Open with a personal admission or contrarian statement
- Use a 3-5 item numbered structure
- Focus on tactical/how-to content over abstract opinion
- Run 150-250 words on LinkedIn, or 5-7 tweets on a thread
- Post Tuesday-Thursday mornings
```

## Step 5: Layer Your Voice

Apply the structural patterns, but fill them with:
- Your own stories and data, not the source creator's
- Your brand voice and terminology
- Your audience's specific pain points

The goal is to adopt *structure*, not to copy *content*. Two posts can share the exact same skeleton (hook type, format, length) while saying completely different things.

## Step 6: Convert

Patterns that drive engagement don't always drive business outcomes. Track:
- Which formats/topics that perform well on engagement also drive profile visits, link clicks, or DMs
- Whether high-engagement posts correlate with follower growth or just temporary reach spikes

Adjust the playbook over time to weight toward patterns that convert, not just patterns that get likes.

## Cadence

Re-run this analysis every 4-8 weeks. Platforms and audience tastes shift — a playbook built from last quarter's top posts can go stale.
