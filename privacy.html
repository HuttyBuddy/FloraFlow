🌿 FloraFlow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 7, 2026

HuttyBuddy ("we") operates FloraFlow: Cultivating Calm through Mindful Gardening (package: com.aistudio.dreamgardendesigner.fhqpvw). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

The following is stored exclusively in an encrypted on-device SQLite database (Android Room) and never transmitted to our servers:

  • Garden designs, companion plant layouts, greenhouse cultivar records
  • Mood and wellbeing journal entries
  • Neural Load Assessment quiz responses and scores
  • Eco-Acoustic Restoration Journal entries and soundscape session logs
  • Plant care schedules and reminder preferences
  • Home screen widget configuration
  • App settings and preferences

2. AI Plant Advisor and Camera/Photo Access

FloraFlow uses the Google Gemini API for plant identification, disease/pest diagnosis, and gardening advice. When you use the AI Advisor:

  • Photos or text you choose to submit are sent directly to Google Gemini API over HTTPS.
  • Free-tier users: up to 3 free trial photo/text queries. Premium subscribers have unlimited access.
  • We do not store your photos or chat history on our servers. Chat history is stored locally only.
  • We never access your camera or photo library without your explicit action.
  • Google's data practices: Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoke camera/photo permissions anytime: Settings → Apps → FloraFlow → Permissions.

3. Mood and Wellbeing Data

Mood and wellbeing journal data is stored exclusively on your device and never transmitted. Delete anytime via in-app controls or Settings → Apps → FloraFlow → Clear Data.

4. Neural Load Assessment

Quiz responses and scores are stored only on-device and used solely to personalize in-app recommendations (e.g., suggesting calmer activities when load is high). Never transmitted. Not a medical or clinical tool.

5. Binaural Soundscapes and Eco-Acoustic Restoration Journal

Audio is bundled within the app - no external streaming required. We do not access your microphone. Eco-Acoustic Restoration Journal entries are stored locally only and never transmitted.

6. Home Screen Widget

The widget reads your local garden data only - no network requests are made by the widget. Configuration is stored locally.

7. Care Reminders and Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device - not from an external server. Disable anytime: Settings → Apps → FloraFlow → Notifications, or in-app settings.

8. Subscriptions and Billing (Free and Premium Tiers)

FloraFlow offers a free tier and optional premium subscriptions. All billing is via Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details - only a purchase token for verification. Free tier: 3 AI Advisor queries trial limit. Manage subscriptions through your Google Play account. Google Payments Privacy Notice: link.

9. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services - distribution and subscription management. Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API - AI plant advice and photo analysis. Terms
  • Google Play Billing - payments. Privacy Notice
  • Google Forms (optional) - user feedback (email, rating, comments). Privacy Policy

10. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All app data: Settings → Apps → FloraFlow → Storage → Clear Data
  • Individual records: in-app deletion controls in each feature
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data

For Google Forms feedback deletion, email us and we will request deletion from Google on your behalf.

11. Security

On-device data is encrypted (Android Room). All API communications use HTTPS/TLS. We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

12. Children's Privacy

FloraFlow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided us personal data.

13. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to access, delete, correct, or opt out of any personal data we hold. Contact us to exercise these rights.

14. Changes to This Policy

We will update the "Last Updated" date when this policy changes. For significant changes we will provide in-app notice. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
