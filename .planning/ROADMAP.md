# Roadmap: FloraFlow App Redesign

## Overview

Redesign FloraFlow to bridge the gap between content promise and app experience by introducing a Neural Load Assessment flow, reframing the AI Advisor to focus on biophilic design, adding post-launch re-engagement widgets and progress tracking, and laying the groundwork for premium visual features.

## Phases

**Phase Numbering:**
- Integer phases (1, 2, 3): Planned milestone work
- Decimal phases (2.1, 2.2): Urgent insertions (marked with INSERTED)

- [ ] **Phase 1: Neural Load Assessment & AI Advisor (P0)** - Implement the 10-question assessment flow, score reveal, next steps, and biophilic advisor mode.
- [ ] **Phase 2: Post-Launch Re-engagement & Diagnosis (P1)** - Implement the home screen widget, conversational Space Diagnosis, 30-day reassessment prompt, and score history.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Premium Experience & AR Relaunch (P2)** - Relaunch AR features with realistic plant rendering, photo space analysis, and community score sharing.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Neural Load Assessment & AI Advisor (P0)
**Goal**: Launch the assessment flow, score results, personalized action items, biophilic design advisor mode, and remove temporary AR icons.
**Depends on**: Nothing
**Requirements**: [ASSESS-01, RESULT-01, STEPS-01, ADVISOR-01, AR-01]
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. User can take a 10-question Neural Load Assessment immediately upon launching the app (no signup block).
  2. User sees a dedicated Score Result screen displaying their score, zone (Green/Yellow/Red), and a customized summary.
  3. User can see 3 personalized next steps with CTAs that link to existing app features (Planner, Advisor, Database).
  4. AI Advisor supports a Biophilic Design mode that references the user's assessment scores in its advice.
  5. The temporary AR animated icons are removed or hidden from the interface.
**Plans**: 1 plan

Plans:

### Phase 2: Post-Launch Re-engagement & Diagnosis (P1)
**Goal**: Drive user retention through home screen widgets, detailed conversational diagnoses, reassessment prompts, and score history.
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: [WIDGET-01, DIAG-01, REASSESS-01, HISTORY-01]
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. User sees a compact, persistent score widget on the home screen showing their Neural Load and step progress.
  2. User can trigger a conversational Space Diagnosis mode where the AI guides them through detailed recommendations.
  3. User receives a prompt to retake the assessment every 30 days.
  4. User can view their historical assessment scores and progress over time in a chart or list.
**Plans**: 1 plan

Plans:
- [ ] 02-01: Implement home screen widgets, history tracking, and conversational space diagnosis.

### Phase 3: Premium Experience & AR Relaunch (P2)
**Goal**: Launch premium differentiator features including photorealistic AR rendering, photo-based space scoring, and community sharing.
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: [AR-02, PHOTO-01, COMM-01]
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. User can visualize photorealistic plant models in AR.
  2. User can upload a photo of their space to automatically generate a Neural Load score.
  3. User can share their before/after environment scores in a community feed.
